# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Tessera aims to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their background, experience level or identity.
Participants agree to make interactions respectful, constructive and harassment-free.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give and receive feedback on code and ideas, not on people.
- Accept that maintainers make final decisions about the project's direction.
- Help newcomers find their way.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory language or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.
- Any conduct a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer, [jullewz](https://github.com/jullewz), through GitHub. Reports are
handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove content, or block participants, that violate this code.
